Butterflies and weather

Does the weather change when UK butterflies appear, and how many there are? I'm joining UK Butterfly Monitoring Scheme counts with historical weather data to test simple hypotheses, starting with one I wrote down before looking at the data: in warmer springs, butterflies fly earlier.

  • Database schema and raw SQL designed by hand, no ORM
  • Linear regression written from scratch in NumPy, then checked against scikit-learn and PyTorch
  • Tested on held-out years and shuffled weather, so it can't learn from the future or fit noise

Next step: exploring the UKBMS data on GBIF. Started November 2026; version 1 has 7 milestones.

Finding patterns in how bees move

Watching bees by hand to label every behaviour doesn't scale. I trained a neural network to learn the shape of their movement on its own, then grouped similar movements to see which patterns emerged.

  • Cleaned, segmented, interpolated and standardised 3D movement trajectories from 50 bees
  • Trained a convolutional autoencoder in PyTorch on the unlabelled data
  • Clustered the learned features with MiniBatch K-means and checked them with silhouette scores, including where the biology stops supporting the maths
